## 1. Summary

Release v0.1 is the portal's first working release. It establishes the initial Service Module (ERP), CRM, R&D Tools, Purchase, and standalone Purchase Requisition (P2P) modules, together with Microsoft SSO, Microsoft Teams integration, SharePoint-backed document storage, and the first security-hardening measures.

The release establishes the core architecture that the portal continues to use: a modular monolith with role- and application-based access control.

---

## 2. What Shipped

### 2.1 Core Modules

* **Service Module (ERP)** — Projects and Service Requests, materials tracking, private project attachments, and warranty/attachment tabs.
* **CRM** — Organizations, Contacts, Inquiries, Tenders, and Activities.
* **R&D Tools** — Railway engineering calculators including braking, hydraulic, load distribution, Qmax, spline, tractive effort, and vehicle performance, with calculation history.
* **Purchase** — Procurement workflow for materials originating from Service Requests, including Requisitions, RFQs, Orders, GRN, Invoices, and Vendors.
* **Purchase Requisition / P2P** — Independent requisition workflow allowing departments to raise purchase requests without an ERP Service Request (`5e74a18`).

### 2.2 CRM Improvements

* Added Word-based Minutes of Meeting export.
* Added PDF export for Minutes of Meeting.
* Added organization creation and editing from CRM pages.
* Added organization and contact context to Activities.
* Added searchable Inquiry/Tender selection to Activity creation.
* Added inline contact creation.
* Added Activity follow-up reminder notifications.
* Added Activity photo support through camera capture and drag-and-drop upload.
* Organization Activities now include activities associated with its Inquiries and Tenders.

### 2.3 Purchase Improvements

* Added remarks to Purchase Requisition materials.
* Added material photo viewing through the Service Request material gallery.
* Added manual Purchase Requisition status overrides.
* Materials are automatically marked as issued after full receipt.

### 2.4 Platform and Access Control

* Added server-side enforcement of ERP sub-permissions.
* Added frontend visibility controls for unauthorized navigation and actions.
* Added Azure AD synchronization from Users & Roles.
* Added Microsoft-account switching during login.

### 2.5 Microsoft Teams

* Added Teams notification support.
* Updated Teams app manifest to version 1.4.2.
* Added updated Teams app icon.
* Added camera and media permissions for Teams mobile and desktop.

### 2.6 Feedback
