# Release v0.4 — CRM Activity & Approval-Role Groundwork

**Module:** Release Notes
**Prepared by:** Vineet Sharma
**Date:** 29 August 2026
**Covers:** 2026-08-24 (`5633d12`, "CRM updates")

---

## 1. Summary

A CRM- and dashboard-focused release. CRM Activities gain a subject field and Word-based Minutes of Meeting export, the dashboard and sidebar receive a visual refresh, and the `User` model gains the `is_department_head` flag required by the v0.5 multi-head P2P approval flow.

---

## 2. What Shipped

### 2.1 CRM

* Added a **subject** field to CRM Activities.
* Added a Word (`.docx`) Minutes of Meeting export generator through `mom_docx.py`.
* Reworked the Activity form and Inquiry Detail panel for clearer activity editing and viewing.
* Added an Activity View dialog.
* Added a reusable `RichTextEditor.tsx` component for CRM forms.

### 2.2 Platform

* Added `is_department_head` to the `User` model.
* This field supports the approval-role model and the multi-head P2P approval routing introduced in v0.5.

### 2.3 P2P

* Updated P2P request routes.
* Updated the New PR and request-detail pages in preparation for the multi-head approval workflow.

### 2.4 UI / Dashboard

* Refreshed the Dashboard layout.
* Refreshed the Sidebar layout.
* Added the `ScrollRevealObserver` component for scroll-triggered UI effects.

---

## 3. Notes on This Document

This release is a direct precursor to v0.5's multi-head P2P approval flow.

The `is_department_head` field and P2P route changes introduced here do not provide the complete approval workflow independently; v0.5 wires the full approval chain around this groundwork.
