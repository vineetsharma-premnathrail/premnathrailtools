# Release v0.5 — Quotation PDF Export & Multi-Head P2P Approvals

**Module:** Release Notes
**Prepared by:** Vineet Sharma
**Date:** 29 August 2026
**Covers:** 2026-08-25 (`957bf5f`)

---

## 1. Summary

CRM gains a complete quotation PDF export with GST breakup, while P2P introduces the multi-head approval workflow: Department Head, Project Head, and Plant Head approval for Purchase Requisitions, followed by the foundation for Purchase Head, Director, and MD approval of the resulting Purchase Order.

---

## 2. What Shipped

### 2.1 CRM — Quotation PDF Export

* Added a quotation PDF generator (`quotation_pdf.py`) built with ReportLab.
* Added a full-width letterhead header and footer.
* Added automatic taxable-value and GST calculations.
* Added CGST/SGST versus IGST breakup based on the quotation's `gst_type`.
* Automatically retrieves customer address and GSTIN from the linked organization.
* Added a signature block.
* Added automatic line-item subtotal and total calculations.
* Automatically populates client, contact, and quotation-date fields.
* Added a **Download PDF** action to the Quotations tab.
* Added **Created Date** and **Created By** columns to the Organizations list.

### 2.2 P2P — Multi-Head Approval Flow

* Added approval-related model and schema changes to `P2PRequest`.
* Added approval comments support.
* Added `is_project_head` and `is_plant_head` fields to `User`.
* Integrated these fields with the existing `is_department_head` field introduced in v0.4.
* Added routes and New PR form support for searching and selecting the Department Head, Project Head, and Plant Head for an individual request.
* A P2P request now reaches **approved** status only after every assigned approval role has signed off.

---

## 3. Notes on This Document

The Purchase Order approval chain was not fully implemented in this release.

The **Purchase Head** and **Director** roles were introduced through `is_purchase_head` and `is_director` in v0.6, while the **MD** role was introduced through `is_md` in v0.7.

See the corresponding release documents for the implementation timeline of those approval roles.
