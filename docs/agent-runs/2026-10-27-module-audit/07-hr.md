## High

Fix: add an "open work in other modules" section, then reassign it or block the exit. (verify)

## Medium

3. No-login workers who report to the leaver (`HrEmployeeProfile.reporting_manager_id`) are never reassigned (lifecycle.py:876-890).
4. PAN, Aadhaar-4, UAN, ESIC, phone and addresses are stored unmasked in the audit log, which bypasses the HR statutory-ID masking (core/audit_registry.py:286).
5. Unpaid leave (LWP) creates a negative balance row that looks overdrawn (routes/leave.py:595-623).
6. The half-day overlap check rejects valid requests: a multi-day leave ending first-half plus a second-half leave on the same day are flagged as overlapping (services/leave.py:186-194).
7. HR cannot curtail leave that has already started (early return); attendance stays "On leave" and the balance needs a hand fix (routes/leave.py:752-760).
8. Leave and regularization lists use a different data-scope rule from the balances and employees lists (routes/leave.py:397 vs :188).

## Low

11. On exit, the leaver's own pending overtime is not cancelled and the legacy OT approver_id is not reassigned. (verify)
12. The employee profile, lifecycle-new and safety-new screens are full pages, not FormSheet (rule 13). Verify whether this is an accepted exception.
13. The employees list defaults to name order, not employee code (rule 14).
14. Carry-forward does not follow changes to last year's balance made after the allotment.
