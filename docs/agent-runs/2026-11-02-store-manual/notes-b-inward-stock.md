## 2. Documents and screens

### 2.1 Stock buckets and how value works (read this first)

Each (item, store) has: On hand (Free), Reserved, Available = On hand - Reserved, Quality, Blocked, With vendor. Reserved stock is part of On hand (it is earmarked, not added). Only Free (minus Reserved) can be issued, transferred or reserved.

Total physically in the store = Free + Quality + Blocked (+ With vendor if sent out on job work, still ours).

Stock value: one weighted (moving) average per item for the whole company (not per store). Receipts are valued at the PO rate (excl. GST) plus planned delivery charges per unit (landed rate), entered into value the moment the goods go into Quality stock. Moving between Free / Quality / Blocked does not change value. Issues go out at the current average. Quality + Blocked + With vendor count in stock value. A line with no price goes in "unvalued" (cost source none) and carries no value. A zero rate is only accepted as a deliberate manual rate with a remark. Valuation depends on Store setting `valuation_enabled` (default OFF in code, switched on by the valuation cut-over). **Confirm it is ON in production before writing the value sections** (open question Q1).

Rupee figures everywhere in Store are hidden (shown as blank / "-") unless the role has feature "Stock value & rates" (`items.stock_values` view).

Stock cannot be posted into a closed Finance period or closed/locked financial year. Error text: "Period <Mon YYYY> is closed in Finance > Period Close, so stock can't be moved on <date> - post with today's date, or ask Finance to reopen <Mon YYYY> if the movement really belongs there." (and a similar one for a closed FY: "Stock can't be moved on <date>: ... Post the movement with today's date instead.")

Stock cannot be posted for an item that is being physically counted (see 2.9): "'<item>' in <store> is being physically counted (PC-...) - its stock movements are on hold until that count is submitted or cancelled (Store > Physical Count)."

Stock cannot be received into an inactive store: "Store '<name>' is inactive, so stock can't be received into it - reactivate it under Store > Settings > Stores, or pick another store."

### 2.2 GRN (Goods Receipt Note) - list

Heading: "Goods Receipt (GRN) & Inspection". Sub-text "Record goods that arrive against a purchase order, then inspect them into Free stock."

Buckets (segment buttons, count in brackets):
- **Upcoming** (admin / Store / Purchase users only): approved POs with quantity still pending. Columns: PO Number, PR Number, PO Date, Vendor, Expected Delivery, Pending from Vendor (per item "name: pending of ordered unit"), Status (Awaiting Delivery = issued/acknowledged, Partially Received = partially_fulfilled), button "Receive ->" which opens the new-GRN form with that PO already picked.
- **Pending Inspection** = GRNs with status `draft`.
- **Completed** = status `completed`.
- **All** (includes Reversed).
Table columns for GRN buckets: GRN Number, PO Number, PO Date, Vendor, Received Date, Status. Sorted newest first (server `created_at` desc), not by number. Click a row to open.
Empty texts: Upcoming "No approved purchase orders are awaiting delivery - issued POs appear here until their goods arrive."; Pending "No goods receipts are waiting for inspection."; Completed "No completed goods receipts yet."; All "No goods receipts recorded yet - receive a delivery from the Upcoming tab or + New Goods Receipt."

### 2.3 GRN lifecycle (statuses)

| Status (label on screen) | DB value | How it gets there | What can happen next |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pending Inspection | `draft` | "Save Goods Receipt" (stock already in QUALITY bucket at this moment) | Complete Inspection -> Completed; Reverse GRN -> Reversed; Quality lot decisions can complete it |
| Completed | `completed` | Every line decided (store inspection, or Quality lot usage decision, or a mix) | Return to Vendor (goods that really came and go back); Reverse GRN (only if untouched downstream) |
| Reversed | `reversed` | Reverse GRN (wrong entry) | Final. Does not count as received; PO opens again |

Line quality status (column "Quality Status"): `pending` ("Pending"), `passed` ("Passed"), `failed` ("Failed"), `partial` ("Partially Accepted"), `in_quality` ("In Quality inspection": item has "Inspection on receipt" ticked, an Inspection Lot decides).

PO status roll-up (done automatically when a GRN completes or is reversed / returned): no accepted qty -> PO `issued`; some -> `partially_fulfilled`; all ordered qty accepted -> `fulfilled`. Linked requisition (PR) status is rolled up from all its POs; the requester gets a bell: "Goods received on <PR>: <GRN> on PO <PO>." when a GRN completes.

There is NO edit of a saved GRN. A wrong GRN is undone only by Reverse GRN (and re-entered).

### 2.4 GRN - create ("Record Goods Receipt")

Who: admin, Store or Purchase users with `grn` create. (Page message otherwise: "Only Store or Purchase users can record a goods receipt - ask an admin to give you Store access.") Store keepers can only receive into stores of their own plant (data scope); Purchase users are cross-plant.

How a PO is picked: the "Purchase Order *" box lists only POs that (a) have status issued / acknowledged / partially_fulfilled, (b) have finished their approval (see below), (c) have at least one non-service line. Label shown: `PO-no - Vendor (PR-no)`. Placeholder "Search a purchase order awaiting receipt...". Service lines are never on a GRN (confirmed on a service entry under the PO).

PO approval gate (backend refuses with a reason): POs uploaded from Tally need no portal approval; a PO with its own approval must be fully released ("approvers and a Partner"). Example messages: "PO '<no>' is still waiting for its approvers / was rejected / was cancelled. Goods can be received against it once its own approval is complete (its approvers and a Partner)..."; for a closed requisition: "Requisition <no> was closed on <date> - goods can't be received on it, and a closed requisition can't be reopened. Raise a new requisition for what is still needed."; PO with no PR: "PO '<no>' isn't linked to a purchase requisition, so it never went through PO approval - goods can't be received against it."

#### 2.4.1 What saving a GRN does to stock (verified)
Every stock line goes into stock immediately, never "after inspection":
1. Item has **Inspection on receipt** ticked in Item Master -> a Quality inspection lot (`IL-YYYY-nnnn`, one lot per heat for batch items) is created, goods go into the **Quality** bucket (ledger type "GRN into Quality" / `quality_in`), line shows "In Quality inspection", remark "Incoming inspection lot IL-...". A first-article (FAI) lot is opened instead when the supplier/item is due for first article. Quality inspectors get a bell "Inspection Lot Waiting".
2. Any other stock line -> goods go into **Quality** bucket too (`quality_in`) with the GRN number as reference; the **GRN inspection** then moves accepted to Free and rejected to Blocked. Store role gets bell "GRN <no> waiting for inspection ... They sit in Quality stock until the inspection is completed in Store > GRN & Inspection."
3. PO line with category **asset**: received and inspected on the GRN but never stock quantity/value ("Finance capitalises it under Finance > Fixed Assets when the vendor's invoice is booked").
Value is entered at the PO rate (+ planned delivery cost) at step 1/2. Putaway bin (if picked) is recorded on the stock row.
A receipt that the ledger refuses (frozen item, closed period, inactive store, needs a rate) is rolled back with the real reason: "'<item>' can't be received into stock, so the GRN was not saved: <reason>".

### 2.5 GRN - detail, inspection, return to vendor, reversal

Header buttons (right): "Return to Vendor" (needs `grn.return_to_vendor` create and at least one decided line with quantity left), "Reverse GRN" (needs `grn.reverse` approve; hidden when any return to vendor exists or GRN already reversed), "<- Back".
Card "Delivery Documents" shows gate entry (no. + date-time), vendor invoice (no. dt. date), delivery challan, e-way bill, vehicle/transporter, LR no.

**Quality Inspection** table (status Pending Inspection) / **Inspection Result** (afterwards). Columns: Item (code, name, unit, bin, serial list), Ordered, Invoice Qty, Received (with "Short n vs bill" / "Excess n vs bill"), Accepted, Rejected, Returned ("n accepted . n rejected"), Quality Status, Rejection Reason.
- Who can inspect: feature `grn.inspection` approve (the permission matrix alone; no named approver). Store keepers with that grant complete a non-QC receipt themselves. Without it the page says: "The goods are already in this store's Quality stock (not issuable). Someone with 'Approve' on Store > GRN & Inspection completes the inspection - accepted quantity then moves to Free stock, rejected to Blocked stock until it goes back to the vendor."
- Per line (only lines with status Pending): Accepted (default = received), Rejected (default 0), Quality Status (Passed / Failed / Partially Accepted), Rejection Reason (free text, shown unless Passed). Serial lines with Partial: tap the rejected serial numbers.
- Rules: Accepted + Rejected must equal received: "Accepted (a) + rejected (r) for '<item>' must equal the n received - adjust the split."; Passed with rejected > 0: "'<item>' is marked Passed but has n rejected - choose Partial, or set rejected to 0."; Failed with accepted > 0: "... choose Partial, or set accepted to 0."; Partial needs both: "'<item>' is marked Partial - it needs both an accepted and a rejected quantity. Otherwise choose Passed or Failed."; negatives refused; a line already inspected cannot be inspected twice; a line "In Quality inspection": "'<item>' is under Quality inspection (<lot>) - its accepted/rejected split comes from the lot's usage decision in Quality > Inspection Lots, not from the GRN."; reversed GRN: "GRN <no> was reversed, so it can't be inspected."; already completed: "This goods receipt has already been completed". Multi-heat line partly accepted: must give the accepted quantity per heat ("... came in N heats (...) and is only partly accepted - enter the accepted quantity per heat."). Serial line partly rejected without naming units: "'<item>' is serial-numbered and n unit(s) were rejected - name the rejected ...".
- Button "Complete Inspection" ("Completing..."). Effect: accepted -> Quality to **Free** (ledger "Quality -> Free"), rejected -> **Blocked** (ledger "Quality -> Blocked"), GRN becomes Completed when no line is pending/in-quality; PO and PR status roll up; requester notified; Store role bell "GRN <no> inspected - all accepted" or "- n line(s) rejected" ("... in Blocked stock until it goes back to the vendor (Return to Vendor)"). If some lines are still with Quality lots, those lines stay "In Quality inspection" and the GRN stays Pending until Quality decides them. A ledger refusal rolls back the whole inspection: "GRN <no> can't be put into stock, so the inspection was not saved: <reason>".
- Amber box "Stock was not fully updated" lists lines that could not be posted, with button "+ Add Item to Item Master".

**Inspection on receipt and the MTC rule (Quality side, for the manual):** On an Inspection Lot the usage decision is Accept / Accept with deviation / Reject / Partial. If the Item Master has "Test certificate (MTC) required" (only selectable when "Inspection on receipt" is ticked) the lot cannot be accepted until the certificate is marked received with its number: "<item> needs a test certificate / MTC before it can be accepted. Mark the certificate received (with its number) on this lot, or reject the lot." Entering a heat's "Mill TC no." on the GRN only pre-fills the lot's certificate reference; it does not mark the certificate received. A critical failed characteristic can never be accepted; Accept-with-deviation needs a deviation reference and a Quality head (not the person who recorded results). The lot decision moves Quality -> Free / Blocked and writes accepted/rejected back on the GRN line.

| Vehicle No. | Optional | |
| Remarks | Optional; required when reason is Other: "Reason is 'Other' - write in the remarks why the goods are going back." | |
Other checks: line must already be inspected ("'<item>' hasn't been inspected yet - returns are made after the accept / reject decision."); not on a reversed GRN. Button "Save Return". Success dialog: "RTV-... saved - the goods are out of stock and the PO is open again. <debit note message>". **Reverse GRN** - prompt "Reverse <GRN>?" with text "Use this only when the GRN was entered by mistake (wrong PO, wrong quantity, wrong store). The accepted stock is taken back out at the PO rate and the PO is open again. Goods that really came and must go back use Return to Vendor instead." Reason required (placeholder "Why is it being reversed? (e.g. entered against the wrong PO)"); button "Reverse GRN". Rejected material is sent back through its NCR; accepted material through Return to Vendor."), if anything was returned, if the vendor's invoice already covers these goods ("The vendor's invoice on PO <no> already covers these goods (n invoiced; only m would stay received). Reversing would leave goods paid for but not received. Use Return to Vendor instead - it raises the debit note for Finance."), or if issued serials remain; if stock was already issued: "Can't take '<item>' (n unit) back out of stock: <reason> Some of it has probably been issued already - get it returned to store first, or use Return to Vendor for what is left." Success: "<GRN> reversed - its stock was taken back out and the PO is open again."

### 2.6 What Finance sees afterwards (3-way match)
Finance books the vendor invoice against the PO line. "Received" for the match = accepted quantity still with us (GRN lines decided, minus Return to Vendor) + accepted service entries; reversed GRNs and undecided (not yet inspected) lines do not count. Invoicing more than received beyond the quantity tolerance, or a price outside the % / rupee tolerance vs the PO rate, is a "variance" needing the Finance Manager's 3-way-match approval. Tolerances live in Procurement > Settings > Invoice match tolerances. So: a GRN must be inspected (Completed) before its goods can be billed, and a Return to Vendor lowers what can be billed. The vendor invoice no. / date / challan / e-way bill typed on the GRN are the receipt-side record that Finance and an auditor compare with the bill; the same invoice number cannot be received twice.

### 2.7 Before PO receipt
Use when material physically arrives but no PO exists yet. Nothing posts to stock. Page heading "Before PO"; red/orange banner "Goods recorded here are held in store and are NOT in stock yet. Stock is added by the GRN once Procurement's PO is approved."

Statuses: Pending Approval (`pending_approval`), Approved, Rejected, Withdrawn (`cancelled`); list pill adds " . your action" when you can decide it. ### 2.9 Physical (cycle) count

Detail: lines Item, UOM, Book (shows "hidden" when blind and you lack Adjustments > Approve), Counted (input), First count (shown after a recount), Difference, Remarks. ### 2.10 Stock Adjustments

### 2.12 Reorder
Heading "Reorder". Lists active Item Master items whose projected stock is at or below their reorder level (or below minimum stock if no reorder level). Raise a purchase requisition or a transfer (Store > Reorder).").

## 4. Realistic storekeeper day (12 steps, suggested sample data)
Sample data to create first (or use existing): vendor "Shree Steel Traders" (Active, Procurement > Vendors); items - "MS Plate 12mm IS2062" (batch tracked, inspection on receipt, MTC required, UoM kg), "Hex Bolt M16x60" (no tracking, UoM nos), "Hydraulic Pump 25 cc" (serial tracked, UoM nos); store "Main Store" with bins R1, R1-S1, R1-S1-B1; PO-2026-xxxx approved for 500 kg plate + 200 bolts + 2 pumps; a Store Manager user as approver.

1. **Morning - see what is due.** Inward > GRN & Inspection > Upcoming: find the PO with "Pending from Vendor" lines and expected delivery today.

## 5. Permissions quick table for the manual (who can do what)
- Record a GRN: Store or Purchase module + `grn` create (+ store in your plant unless Purchase team).
- Inspect a GRN / release or block a Quality serial: `grn.inspection` approve.
- Return to Vendor: `grn.return_to_vendor` create; see the returns list: view.
- Reverse a GRN: `grn.reverse` approve AND Purchase module.
- Attach mill TC: `grn.mill_tc` create. Block/Release batch: `adjustments.batch_status` edit.
- Before PO: `grn` view / create.
- Material return: `returns` create; approvers come from the workflow.
- Adjustment: `adjustments` create; approver needs `adjustments` approve and the Store Manager flag (or be the store's manager); reject = `adjustments` reject.
- Physical count: `adjustments.cycle_count` view/create; seeing book qty on a blind count needs `adjustments` approve.
- Opening stock import: `adjustments` create. Clear blocked stock: `adjustments.quarantine_clear` create.
- Bins: `bins` create/edit/delete; moves: `transfers.bin_moves` create.
- Stock on a date: `stock.as_of`. Money: `items.stock_values` view. Exports: `stock` / `movements` export.
- Data scope: Store users see only stores of their plant (Organization > Role & Permissions data access); Purchase team users are cross-plant for GRNs.

## 6. Surprises worth knowing
1. A GRN posts stock immediately into Quality stock; "Complete Inspection" only RELEASES it to Free (accepted) or Blocked (rejected). Old text saying stock is added "after inspection" is wrong.
2. "Returns" under Inward is the Material Return (job to store). The vendor return is a button on the GRN.
3. A saved GRN cannot be edited, only reversed (Purchase team) and re-entered.
4. Over-receipt is blocked with no tolerance; short receipts keep the PO open.
5. Opening Stock Import is open to anyone with Adjustments > Create (not admin only).
