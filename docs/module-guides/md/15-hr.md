# HR & Administration Module

The HR & Administration module (app key "hr") is where the company keeps its people records and runs the day-to-day HR and admin services: employee profiles and documents, the reporting line (org chart), joining / confirmation / transfer / promotion / exit events with their checklists, leave, attendance, the holiday calendar, company assets, the visitor gate register, business travel requests and expense claims. Every employee also uses it as a self-service area ("My HR") to see their own record, apply for leave, check in and out, raise travel requests and expense claims, and see the assets issued to them. Payroll is NOT run here: salaries stay in ADP, and the portal only supplies the monthly attendance summary and records the reimbursement payment reference. HR is a supporting module rather than one step of the core business flow (Customer → BD → R&D → Design/EHP → BOM → Store/Purchase → Production → QC → Dispatch → Commissioning → Machine → Service → Finance). It feeds every step of that flow with the people data the other modules depend on: the reporting manager on each profile decides who approves leave, attendance, travel and expense requests; department, designation and plant set on the HR profile are copied to the person's portal account so approvals in other modules (for example P2P department-head routing) follow them; and an exit hands the leaver's approvals, P2P buyer work, reportees and department head slots to a successor and switches off their portal access.

## 2. Dashboard

Route: /dashboard/hr. HR team only: needs the hr app and view on HR › Dashboard (backend gate require_hr_tab("dashboard")). A non-HR user who opens it is redirected to /dashboard/hr/me. Heading "HR Dashboard", subtitle "As of [today] (IST). Headcount, joiners and leavers, leave and attendance today, pending approvals." All numbers are calculated live by the backend (services/dashboard.py) when the page loads; "today" and "this month" are India time. Every number is limited to the viewer's HR data access (Organization › Role & Permissions → Data access). The "headcount population" is every active portal user who is a real person (not a service account) and is not marked exited in HR.

**Actions (header):** **↻ Refresh** reloads all numbers. If loading fails a red box shows the real error.

#### Create form (Apply for leave — shared LeaveApplyForm)

| Field | Type | Required | Options / source | Rules & validation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Leave type | dropdown | Yes | Active leave types, "Name (CODE)", unpaid ones marked "— unpaid"; defaults to the first | Type must be active |
| From | date | Yes | — | — |
| Session / Start session | dropdown | Yes (default Full day) | Single day: Full day, First half, Second half. Multi-day: Full day, From second half | Half-day choices hidden when the type does not allow half days. A multi-day leave cannot start with only the first half |
| To | date | No (blank = same as From) | — | Must be on/after From; same calendar year as From; at most one year long |
| End session | dropdown | Only for multi-day | Full day, Till first half | A multi-day leave cannot end with only the second half |
| Reason | multi-line text | Yes | — | Must not be blank |
| Contact during leave | text | No | — | — |
| Supporting document | file | Required only when the type needs it | Images, PDF, Word | Required when the leave is longer than the type's "Document after (days)"; stored in SharePoint |

Live preview (recalculated by the server as the form changes) shows: number of days, balance available (and days already in pending requests), "No balance needed (unpaid leave)" for unpaid types, the approver ("HR (no reporting manager set)" when no manager), the dates not counted (weekly offs and holidays), and every problem found. **Submit request** is enabled only when the preview has no errors and a reason is entered.

Day counting rules: only working days count. Weekly offs come from the employee's plant working days (Sunday only if the plant's text cannot be read). National and festival holidays that apply to the plant are skipped. If the HR setting "Sandwich rule" is on, offs and holidays lying between two leave days of the same request are charged. Half days count 0.5. Other checks: gender restriction of the type (gender must be recorded on the profile), maximum days at a stretch, required document, no overlap with another pending/approved leave (two different halves of the same single day are allowed), and for paid types enough balance after subtracting days already in pending requests (no balance row = "Ask HR to allot this year's leave").

On submit the request gets number LV-[year]-NNNN, status Pending, the requester's reporting manager is captured as approver, and it is sent to the central approval engine (Organization › Approval Workflows; default: reporting manager plus HR approvers), which notifies the approvers.

#### Statuses & workflow

| Status | Meaning / how it is reached |
|---|---|
| Pending | Just submitted |
| Approved | Approved by the routed approver. On approval the days are recounted against today's calendar, the balance is checked again and deducted, and each leave day is written to attendance as "On leave" (or "Half day" for a half portion). Approval is refused if the balance is no longer enough or if attendance already shows the person working on those days |
| Rejected | Rejected with a mandatory reason; "returned for changes" from the engine also ends the request as Rejected ("Returned for changes: …") |
| Cancelled | Withdrawn by the employee (pending, or approved and not started) or cancelled by HR |

Nobody can approve or reject their own request.

Deactivate stops a holiday counting as a day off (already approved leave is not recalculated). Delete removes it permanently.

### Daily reminders (09:00, with Teams)

Sent by the scheduled job hr_reminders, once per recipient/record/day, as bell plus Microsoft Teams notifications:

- Employee documents expiring in exactly 30 days, 7 days, or today: the employee (not for "HR Only" documents) and every HR user.
- Probation ending within 7 days (onboarding/probation profiles): HR, the reporting manager and the employee — once per probation end date.
- HR approvals pending more than 2 days: the captured approver, or every HR user when there is no approver or the approver cannot act.
- Pending HR requests re-routed to a new manager during a lifecycle change notify the requester (Teams).

Clicking an HR notification in the bell opens: My Leave, My Attendance, My Travel, the claim page, My Assets, Visitors, the lifecycle event, or the HR Dashboard (document and probation reminders); approver notifications ("waiting for you", "Approval needed:", "Escalated:", "Overdue approval:") open Approvals.
